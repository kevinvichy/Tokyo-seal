十年的專業拍攝經驗，大部分都在日本。為旅客拍攝旅拍寫真、和服寫真，也為品牌拍攝商業產品照。每次拍攝都有會說英語的助理同行，當天的溝通和安排都更輕鬆。

Ten years of professional work, most of it here in Japan. Travel portraits for visitors, kimono sessions at the temples, and commercial product photography for brands. Every shoot comes with an English-speaking assistant, so planning and directing stay easy on the day.
